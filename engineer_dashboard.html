System Information

System Name: AI-Enhanced Quality Control System

Version: 2.0

Developed By: Ian T Chigomere (R214155E)

Project Supervisor: Eng Mugwindiri

Description: An automated quality control prediction model based on historical and real-time data, integrated with Six Sigma methodology to achieve high precision defect detection.

Release Date: 2025-03-06

Contact: ian.chigomere@example.com

Objectives
  • Objective 1: Achieve a minimum reduction of at least 2% in the defect rate compared to the current baseline by systematically identifying and addressing the root causes of defects within the manufacturing process.
  • Objective 2: Achieve a reduction in the product rejection rate by 5%, thus enhancing overall quality assurance processes and minimizing waste within the manufacturing framework.
  • Objective 3: Improve customer satisfaction score by 5% regarding product quality.
  • Objective 4: Conduct a financial analysis to ensure the solution is a profitable investment for the business, delivering high returns on implementation.
AI Usage

The system utilizes AI for:

  • Defect Detection: AI algorithms analyze visual data to automatically detect defects such as glass breakages, foreign objects, packaging issues, and labeling errors.
  • Predictive Maintenance: AI models predict machine failures to enable proactive maintenance, reducing downtime and improving efficiency.
  • Quality Prediction: AI forecasts product quality based on various factors, ensuring only high-quality beverages reach the market.
  • Automation: Automation of quality control processes reduces human error and ensures consistent monitoring.
System Architecture

The system follows a three-tier architecture:

  1. Presentation Tier: User interface for engineers and operators.
  2. Application Tier: Business logic and AI algorithms, including descriptive statistics, inferential statistics, root cause analysis, and visualization tools (Minitab, IBM SPSS).
  3. Data Tier: Database for storing production data, defect information, customer feedback, and system logs.

The system is integrated with the Six Sigma methodology to enhance defect detection precision.
